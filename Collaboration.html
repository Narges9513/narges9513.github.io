Industry & Collaboration

I am interested in collaborating with healthcare providers, digital health companies, medical technology businesses, and public-sector organizations to address complex healthcare challenges through data analytics, optimization, and decision modeling.

Healthcare Operations

Exploring opportunities to improve patient pathways, service delivery, capacity planning, and resource allocation through analytical and optimization-based approaches.

Medical Decision-Making

Developing decision-support models for screening, monitoring, treatment, and follow-up strategies that account for clinical uncertainty, patient differences, and practical implementation constraints.

Digital Health and Medical Technologies

Assessing how diagnostic tools, digital health solutions, and emerging medical technologies can be integrated into healthcare pathways to improve clinical outcomes and operational efficiency.

Health Economics and Intervention Evaluation

Evaluating the potential costs, benefits, and cost-effectiveness of healthcare interventions, screening programmes, and prevention strategies to support evidence-informed decisions.

Public Health and Prevention

Applying optimization, simulation, and data analytics to public health challenges, including vaccination, disease prevention, and the allocation of limited healthcare resources.
