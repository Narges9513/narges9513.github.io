Teaching

I teach the following modules to MSc Business Analytics students at Bayes Business School.

Business Analytics Fundamentals

Programme: MSc Business Analytics
Term: Autumn

This module introduces the foundations of business analytics and their role in supporting managerial decision-making. Students explore descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, and prescriptive analytics, learning how to formulate business problems, interpret data, evaluate analytical insights, and develop evidence-based recommendations. The module emphasizes practical applications, business interpretation, and the effective communication of analytical findings.

Data Management and Visualization

Programme: MSc Business Analytics
Term: Autumn

This module introduces the principles and tools needed to manage, analyze, and communicate business data. Students develop practical skills in relational databases, SQL, data modeling, NoSQL databases, and graph-based data structures. The module also explores data visualization and interactive dashboard development using Tableau, helping students transform complex data into clear insights that support business decision-making.
